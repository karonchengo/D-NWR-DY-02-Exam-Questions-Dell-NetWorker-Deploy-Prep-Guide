## D-NWR-DY-02 Exam Objectives and Weighting

The D-NWR-DY-02 exam is divided into three major domains. Understanding their weightings helps candidates allocate study time according to the relative importance of each area.

### Pre-Deployment — 34%

Pre-deployment focuses on evaluating the customer environment and preparing the infrastructure for a successful NetWorker implementation. Candidates should understand how to assess existing systems, gather deployment information, prepare documentation, and determine the appropriate configuration for the intended environment.

Key topics include:
- Analyzing and evaluating the customer environment for NetWorker deployment.
- Preparing deployment documentation.
- Preparing target systems.
- Evaluating NetWorker software sizing and optimization requirements.
- Assessing data protection requirements.

A strong understanding of this domain helps candidates recognize potential deployment risks before installation begins. Preparation should include reviewing infrastructure dependencies, confirming prerequisites, and ensuring that the proposed deployment aligns with the customer's backup and recovery objectives.

### Deployment — 42%

Deployment is the largest exam domain, accounting for 42% of the assessed content. It concentrates on implementing a new NetWorker environment, configuring backup components, and troubleshooting issues that arise during installation and configuration.

Key topics include:
- Deploying the initial software installation for a new NetWorker environment.
- Configuring backup targets.
- Configuring groups and policies.
- Analyzing and troubleshooting deployment issues.

Candidates should understand how deployment planning translates into a functioning backup environment. This includes recognizing the purpose of backup targets, understanding how groups and policies organize protection activities, and identifying configuration problems that could prevent successful deployment.

Because this domain carries the greatest weighting, candidates should dedicate substantial preparation time to installation workflows, configuration dependencies, and scenario-based troubleshooting.

### Post-Deployment — 24%

Post-deployment covers the activities needed to confirm that the NetWorker environment is operational and that its backup and recovery functions perform as intended.

Key topics include:
- Validating the deployment.
- Troubleshooting failed backup and restore operations.
- Creating appropriate documentation.

Candidates should understand why deployment validation is essential rather than assuming that a successful installation guarantees a working data protection solution. Backup jobs, recovery operations, system configuration, and operational readiness all require appropriate verification.

Documentation is also important because it helps support teams understand the deployed environment, investigate issues, and maintain consistent operational procedures.
